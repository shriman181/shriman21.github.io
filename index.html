The problem

The kitchen runs on gut feel. And it's costing you.

Every morning, a sous chef walks in and makes a hundred decisions from memory. How much to thaw. What sold last Tuesday. Whether the new cook is portioning the duck right.

All the data to answer these questions already exists — your POS, inventory, reservations. But none of it talks to each other. The result: 25–35% over-prepped, every single day. And when your head chef leaves, every insight they ever built walks out with them.
